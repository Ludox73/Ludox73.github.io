HyperMarimba — Horocyclic Flow

Ludovico Battista and Juan Souto  |  ← Geodesic flow version

In the geodesic flow version a point moves along geodesics on a genus-2 hyperbolic surface constructed from four right-angled hexagons. Here instead the point moves along horocycles — circles tangent to the boundary of the Poincaré disk at an ideal point ξ. Whenever the horocyclic trajectory crosses one of the three marked curves on the surface, a marimba note is played.

The horocyclic flow is uniquely ergodic: unlike geodesic flow, almost every horocyclic orbit equidistributes with respect to the Liouville measure, producing a distinctly different rhythmic texture. The arcs drawn below (in blue) are arcs of Euclidean circles entirely contained inside the disk — the horocycles of the Poincaré model.
